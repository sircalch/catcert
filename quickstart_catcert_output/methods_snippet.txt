Periodic surface slab calculations for Pt(111) were performed with VASP utilizing the PBE-D3 exchange-correlation functional. Slab thickness convergence, vacuum isolation, work functions, and electrostatic potential profiles were certified using CatCert v1.0.0 (Monreal-Hernández, 2026). Surface energy convergence as a function of slab thickness was audited using Fiorentini-Methfessel linear regression and pointwise layer differences (converged gamma = 0.084 J/m^2, Delta gamma = 0.0000 J/m^2). Periodic image interactions were decoupled with 16.0 Angstroms of vacuum spacing, confirming potential plateau flatness (Delta V_vac = 0.0247 eV, yielding a work function Phi = 5.70 eV). The surface model achieved an overall quality certification status of: PASS.
